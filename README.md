# Full Page Screenshot

**Capture the whole page. Or exactly the part you need.**

A Chrome extension for full page screenshots, area selection that scrolls
with you while you drag, and multi-snip collection across tabs. Every
capture is copied straight to your clipboard, ready to paste with Ctrl+V.

### 🌐 [**Website → axagon79.github.io/full-page-screenshot**](https://axagon79.github.io/full-page-screenshot/)

### 🧩 [**Install from the Chrome Web Store**](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/napeefngooaeinknhnngbnokkadmmomj)

---

## Capture modes

| Mode | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Full Page** | Scrolls the page for you and stitches every part into one image, top to bottom. Works on regular sites and on web apps that scroll inside an internal panel — webmail, dashboards, chat apps. |
| **Select Area** | Drag a rectangle around what you need. Doesn't all fit on the screen? Keep dragging: the page scrolls by itself and the selection keeps growing. That's *scroll selection* — almost no other extension does it. |
| **Visible Only** | Instant screenshot of exactly what's on screen right now. |
| **Multi Snip** | Grab several pieces one after another, even from different tabs, then save them as one stitched image or as separate files. |

## Privacy

Zero data collection, zero tracking, zero analytics. Everything happens on
your device: screenshots go to your Downloads folder and your clipboard,
nowhere else. No account, no sign-up, no cloud upload.
