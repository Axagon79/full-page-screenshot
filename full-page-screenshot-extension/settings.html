    How it works
    Full Page
    Click the icon: it scrolls the page for you and stitches every part into one image, top to bottom.
    Visible Only
    Click the icon: instant screenshot of exactly what's on screen right now, nothing more.
    Select Area
    Click the icon, then drag a rectangle. Drag near the top or bottom edge to auto-scroll — you can select an area taller than the screen.
    After every capture
    The image is saved to a "screenshots" folder inside your Downloads, and — if "Copy to clipboard" is on above — also copied, so you can just press Ctrl+V to paste it anywhere.
    Choosing a mode
    Pick a mode above and hit Save — it becomes the one-click default. Right-click the icon any time to come back here and switch.
